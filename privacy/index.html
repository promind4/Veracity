Veracity Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Veracity is a browser extension that helps users review claims in YouTube videos and explore their content. This policy describes the information involved when using its features.

Information processed

Depending on the features you use, Veracity may process YouTube video identifiers, URLs, titles, channel names, descriptions, captions or transcripts, your questions and chat messages, generated reports and translations, glossary and learning data, settings and API keys that you provide. A learning profile, if supplied, may also be used to adapt explanations.

Local storage and files

Settings, API keys, video-related data, history, reports, chats, translations and learning data may be stored locally in your browser. If you select a folder, Veracity may also save and read documents and backups in that folder. Files saved there are separate from browser storage. Protect your device and your API keys; browser-local storage is not a dedicated secret vault.

Third-party services

When you use an online feature, Veracity sends the information needed for that feature directly to the relevant service. Depending on your settings and the feature, this can include video details, transcript excerpts or full transcripts, claims, search queries, URLs, chat messages and any learning profile you supply. Your API key is sent to the corresponding provider for authentication. For audio transcription or voice generation, video identifiers, text or audio-related data may be sent to the provider used for that feature.

Depending on your configuration, providers may include Google Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Serper, Jina, ScraperAPI and Google Fact Check Tools. YouTube supplies video information and captions. OpenRouter may route requests to the model provider you select. Search or page-reading services may receive the URLs or queries needed to find and retrieve sources. Providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms, which may differ, including in retention and use of API data.

Purpose

Information is used to retrieve and assess video content, search for sources, provide reports, answer questions, offer transcripts, translations and learning tools, and save or restore your work. The information received from Google APIs is used in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data stored in the browser remains there until you remove it using available controls or remove the extension and its local data. Removing the extension does not necessarily delete files you exported or saved in a selected folder; delete those files separately. Third-party providers apply their own retention periods and deletion procedures.

Your choices

You can choose which features to use, which supported services and keys to configure, and whether to select a local folder. You can remove stored video data through available extension controls and remove or replace API keys in settings. Avoid submitting sensitive information in questions or learning profiles. Review the privacy policies of the providers you choose.

Changes and contact

This policy may change when Veracity changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. For privacy questions, contact the project through its GitHub issue page. Issues may be public; do not post API keys, transcripts or other sensitive information there.
